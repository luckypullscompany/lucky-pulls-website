OUR STORY

Built by Alex & Michelle.

Lucky Pulls & Company is a locally owned vending business focused on creating convenient, fun experiences for customers and practical partnerships for host businesses.

We started with the idea of making sealed trading card packs easier to find and expanded the vision to include snack and beverage vending as the company grows.

Our goal is simple: keep machines clean, stocked, dependable, and worth coming back to.

Local-first mindset

We want to grow through strong relationships with local businesses and customers.

Professional presentation

Our machines are designed to look clean, modern, and on-brand in your space.

Long-term partnerships

We aim to build dependable, low-maintenance vending partnerships that are worth keeping.

Frequently asked questions

Simple answers for customers and host locations.

What products will Lucky Pulls machines carry?

Depending on the machine and location, we plan to offer sealed trading card packs, snacks, drinks, and other convenient vending products.

Does a host business need to buy inventory?

No. Lucky Pulls & Company supplies, stocks, maintains, and services the machine.

How does the 10% host payment work?

Our current partnership model offers the host location 10% of the monthly profit generated by the machine placed at that business.

Where are your machines located?

Our first locations are currently being developed. Public locations will be listed on this website as they launch.
