About us

Built by collectors, for customers and local businesses.

We’re Alex and Michelle, the owners of Lucky Pulls & Company. Our goal is to create convenient vending experiences that are fun for customers and genuinely useful for the businesses that host them.

We started with the idea of making sealed trading card packs easier to find, then expanded the vision to include snack and beverage vending as Lucky Pulls grows.

We believe the best vending locations are partnerships: customers get convenience, host businesses get added traffic and revenue potential, and we get to build something local that people enjoy.
